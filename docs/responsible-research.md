# Responsible research

DealSage uses publicly accessible data only and never bypasses authentication, paywalls, CAPTCHAs, access controls, or publisher rules. Source adapters must respect robots directives where applicable, terms, rate limits, and source-specific behavior. APIs, public datasets, RSS, and structured government endpoints are preferred.

The system retains attribution and evidence supporting material findings; distinguishes source evidence from inference; explains automated conclusions; routes ambiguous identities to human review; lets analysts reject and correct matches; and preserves meaningful actions in an audit trail. Humans remain responsible for downstream business actions.

Collect only information relevant to legitimate business research. Avoid unnecessary sensitive data, minimize retention, and protect stored artifacts. A transition signal is not proof of death, ownership, control, willingness to transact, or an opportunity. Analysts must consider the dignity and privacy of affected people and families.
