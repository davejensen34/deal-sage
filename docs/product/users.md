# Users and jobs

- Private-equity and independent-sponsor sourcing teams: discover potential proprietary conversations earlier.
- Family offices and corporate-development teams: maintain a consistent transition-research pipeline.
- M&A advisors and business brokers: identify businesses that may need succession or transaction guidance.
- Research analysts: resolve identity, ownership, timing, and conflicting evidence efficiently.

The primary job is resolving a research case into an honest, reviewable outcome: determine whether credible evidence connects a transition signal, person, business, and control relationship; retain contradictions and unresolved questions; and record a reasoned human conclusion. Analysts may begin from a person or signal, a known business, or independently discovered evidence on both sides. A case may correctly end with no business found, relationship unknown, or insufficient evidence rather than becoming a candidate.
