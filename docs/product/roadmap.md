# Product roadmap

## Milestone 1 — Evidence and Analyst Foundation

Active reconciliation. The persisted fictional-data MVP is implemented; current validation and gaps are tracked in `docs/project/current-state.md` and `backlog/milestones/milestone-1.md`.

## Milestone 2 — Business and Owner Discovery Proof

Prove, in one or two jurisdictions, that public sources can yield credible owner/controller evidence at useful quality, coverage, reliability, and cost. This is an experiment, not national connector expansion.

## Later milestones

3. Real transition-signal discovery and extraction.
4. Identity resolution and bounded research automation.
5. Opportunity intelligence: enrichment, saved research, watchlists, and alerts.
6. Operational productization: authentication, multi-user controls, backups, monitoring, and deployment hardening.
7. Broader transition intelligence: retirement, succession, ownership transfer, founder exit, dissolution, and leadership change.

Advancement depends on evidence from the preceding milestone.
