# Product vision

DealSage helps private-equity firms, independent sponsors, family offices, M&A advisors, business brokers, and corporate-development teams identify privately held businesses where ownership transition may be occurring—and understand why the candidate deserves attention.

The first differentiating signal is a possible recent death of an owner or controlling individual, but the product is a generalized business ownership and transition intelligence platform. It should answer: which business may be approaching transition, who appears to control it, what event may have occurred, how confidently do the identities align, what evidence supports or contradicts the conclusion, and what should an analyst do next?

Success means earlier identification, less repetitive research, stronger sourcing consistency, higher data quality, faster review, and defensible evidence. It does not mean replacing analyst judgment.
