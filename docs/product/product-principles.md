# Product principles

1. Evidence before inference; analyst decision after both.
2. Skeptical by design: insufficient evidence is safer than a confident false match.
3. Ownership discovery, relationship validation, signal discovery, identity resolution, and opportunity confidence are separate problems.
4. Preserve business-first, signal-first, and hybrid discovery strategies until measurements justify narrowing; knowing a business name is never a prerequisite for candidate discovery.
5. Generalize transition signals beyond death without diluting the initial use case.
6. Human review is architecture, not cleanup.
7. Prefer deterministic, explainable behavior and use AI only where unstructured reasoning adds value.
8. Keep development inexpensive, local-first, open-source-first, and operationally simple.
9. Measure research correctness, source quality, analyst burden, reliability, and cost—not vanity metrics.
