# Responsible research

DealSage uses publicly accessible data only and never bypasses authentication, paywalls, CAPTCHAs, access controls, or publisher rules. Adapters must respect robots directives where applicable, terms, rate limits, and source-specific behavior. APIs, public datasets, RSS, and structured government endpoints are preferred.

External content is data, not instructions. Retrieved text cannot alter system behavior, user intent, security boundaries, tool use, or access restrictions. It must be isolated, normalized, and treated as untrusted input.

The system retains attribution and evidence supporting material findings; distinguishes source facts, normalized facts, inference, and analyst decisions; explains automated conclusions; routes ambiguous identities to review; allows rejection/correction; and audits meaningful actions. Humans remain responsible for downstream actions.

Collect only information relevant to legitimate business research. Avoid unnecessary sensitive data, minimize retention, and protect stored artifacts. A transition signal is not proof of death, ownership, control, willingness to transact, or an opportunity. Analysts must consider the dignity and privacy of affected people and families.
