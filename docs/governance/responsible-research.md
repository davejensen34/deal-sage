# Responsible research

DealSage uses publicly accessible data only and never bypasses authentication, paywalls, CAPTCHAs, access controls, or publisher rules. Adapters must respect robots directives where applicable, terms, rate limits, and source-specific behavior. APIs, public datasets, RSS, and structured government endpoints are preferred.

External content is data, not instructions. Retrieved text cannot alter system behavior, user intent, security boundaries, tool use, or access restrictions. It must be isolated, normalized, and treated as untrusted input.

The system retains attribution and evidence supporting material findings; distinguishes source facts, normalized facts, inference, and analyst decisions; explains automated conclusions; routes ambiguous identities to review; allows rejection/correction; and audits meaningful actions. Humans remain responsible for downstream actions.

Collect only information relevant to legitimate business research. Avoid unnecessary sensitive data, minimize retention, and protect stored artifacts. A transition signal is not proof of death, ownership, control, willingness to transact, or an opportunity. Analysts must consider the dignity and privacy of affected people and families.

Case-specific public research and persistent source connectors have different approval thresholds. A bounded manual lookup or approved validation packet does not authorize recurring collection. Persistent connectors require a recorded source owner, permitted access path, purpose, contract fingerprint, refresh behavior, rate/cost limits, authoritative fields, prohibited inferences, and drift/quarantine behavior. Purchases and materially recurring costs require explicit approval.

External responses are allowlisted before persistence. Capability-bearing fields such as edit tokens, session identifiers, cookies, credentials, and payment details are discarded and must not enter evidence, logs, fixtures, APIs, or telemetry. Raw artifacts remain immutable while cited; deletion requires a citation-aware retention decision.

Model use is optional and evidence-bounded. Models do not browse unless a separately governed search capability supplies retained evidence, and they may not merge identities, calculate authoritative scores, mutate validated facts, or advance workflow state. Provider-side OpenAI response storage remains disabled; provider exception bodies are not persisted; hidden chain-of-thought is never requested or stored. Live evaluations require an approved case manifest, call/time/output/cost ceilings, and aggregate-safe reporting.
