## Definition of done

- One selected local database contains current bounded Colorado and Texas source observations plus an exact replay of the retained Utah delivery, with immutable artifacts, lineage, idempotency, and aggregate-safe metrics.
- Real cases may begin signal-first, business-first, or hybrid; no path requires a known entity name as its universal prerequisite.
- Every transition packet is fully retrieved and content-hashed before the manifest is frozen or paid providers run. Search snippets never become evidence.
- Any live model execution has explicit provider/model/case/call/cost approval and receives only qualified landed evidence.
- Deterministic validation and an attributable analyst disposition precede any candidate entering the real review queue.
- Results distinguish credible opportunities from ambiguity, former ownership, unsupported relationships, and no-business outcomes.

## Boundaries

- State source roles remain assertions. Registered agents, applicants, officers, and an `Owner` label do not become validated ownership automatically.
- The existing Utah delivery may be replayed without another purchase; repeat acquisition requires a new cost and reuse decision.
- Colorado and Texas refreshes remain bounded to their permitted public APIs and provide corroboration, not transition discovery or beneficial ownership.
- No paid search or model call is authorized until Issue #87 carries a frozen protocol and explicit approval.
- No autonomous crawling, contact, publication of record-level private evidence, recurring scheduler, alerting, or Milestone 5 activation is included.
