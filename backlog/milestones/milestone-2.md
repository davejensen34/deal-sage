# Milestone 2 — Business and Owner Discovery Proof

Status: proposed; do not implement without user direction.

## Experiment

Determine whether one public jurisdiction can produce credible owner/controller evidence for a representative SMB sample at acceptable quality, access reliability, and near-zero infrastructure cost.

Recommended first comparison: Colorado and Utah, pending current primary-source verification. Choose one after a short access/field/terms review; do not build two adapters by default.

## Acceptance criteria

- A documented, permitted access method and source terms.
- A hand-labeled sample of at least 50 privately held entities across relevant entity types.
- Explicit role taxonomy separating owner/member/manager/officer/registered agent/unknown.
- Measured usable coverage, role precision, stale/ambiguous rate, retrieval success, latency, and marginal cost.
- Raw source artifacts and normalized evidence preserve attribution.
- No candidate or transition-signal automation is required.
- A go/change/stop recommendation based on results.

## Demonstration proof

Given the sample, an analyst can inspect the source record, normalized relationship assertion, provenance, uncertainty, and aggregate experiment measures. No national-coverage claim is made.
